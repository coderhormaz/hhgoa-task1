# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Builders, attendees, and community members discovering HH Goa 2026, primarily through social links and mobile devices. They want to understand the event identity quickly and create a recognizable builder ID or profile-picture frame.

## Product Purpose

Frame In Goa turns a builder's photo and basic profile details into shareable HH Goa 2026 artwork. The home page should communicate the campaign immediately and move people into the generator without friction.

## Brand Personality

Bold, energetic, and distinctly Goan. The experience uses a poster-like editorial voice, strong event colors, expressive display type, and playful builder-culture language.

## Anti-references

Do not turn the experience into a generic SaaS landing page, soften it with rounded cards or muted neutrals, or replace the established green, yellow, pink, and paper visual identity. Avoid mobile layouts that achieve fit by making text illegible or hiding the campaign artwork.

## Design Principles

- Preserve the campaign poster identity at every viewport size.
- Make the primary story legible before decorative details compete for attention.
- Keep generated artwork visible, bounded, and recognizably detailed on mobile.
- Make the path into the generator obvious and comfortable to tap.
- Prefer deliberate mobile recomposition over proportional desktop shrinking.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain strong color contrast, visible keyboard focus, comfortable touch targets, semantic headings and labels, and reduced-motion support. Responsive layouts must remain usable from 320px wide upward without horizontal scrolling.
